**Inves Capital is introducing ZARPY: a yield-bearing companion to the ZARP Stablecoin. Approved partners can earn a share of top-tier money market fund returns for as long as their ZARP is staked; in months, or even minutes. Powered by the Old Mutual Money Market Fund.**

## Short-term Rand has always been left out

Money market funds are where South African institutions keep cash they want safe, kept liquid, and earning yield. Subscriptions and redemptions run on business-day cut-offs and settlement cycles. Even newer digital alternatives usually count yield from a single daily snapshot. Rand that is needed again this afternoon, or that sits between trades overnight, usually earns little or nothing.

ZARPY changes that.

Now, for the first time, Rand value can be put to work in a real-time yield engine. Stake in the morning, remove in the afternoon, with yield. Automate and streamline your capital deployment. Only possible with ZARP Stablecoin, and ZARPY.

## How ZARPY works

- **Stake ZARP, receive ZARPY.** An approved partner stakes ZARP into the ZARPY smart contract and receives ZARPY one-for-one.
- **Yield accrues continuously.** From the moment ZARP is staked, ZARPY accrues yield in real time. The rate is benchmarked to the Old Mutual Money Market Fund, less a service fee. Stake for an hour and you earn an hour's share of the day's yield.
- **Unstake whenever you like.** Return ZARPY at any time and get your ZARP back one-for-one, on-chain, immediately. There is no lock-up and no minimum term.
- **Claim on your schedule.** The yield you have earned is recorded for you automatically and is never forfeited. You claim it as additional ZARPY whenever it suits you.

## Why this is new

Rand-denominated yield is not new. What ZARPY adds is time. Yield accrues second by second, not at an end-of-day snapshot or on a fund's settlement cycle. Idle capital starts earning immediately, and yield can be claimed at any time.

> Institutional treasuries have always had to choose between earning a proper money market rate and keeping their rands on hand. ZARPY removes that trade-off. Your rands earn for every minute they're not working, and it's back in your wallet the moment you need it.

## Built for institutions

ZARPY is a permissioned product for institutions, not a retail or open DeFi product. It is available only to ZARP Partners that Inves Capital has verified and separately approved for ZARPY. Approved partners may stake ZARP however they acquired it.

## Designed with safeguards

- **Fully backed.** Every ZARPY is backed one-for-one by ZARP, with treasury reserves managed by Old Mutual Wealth.
- **Yield tied to real returns.** The smart contract can create yield only up to the returns the reserves have actually earned, as confirmed by independent attestation.
- **Independently checked.** Reserves are [attested independently](/transparency), and smart contracts are audited before being deployed.
