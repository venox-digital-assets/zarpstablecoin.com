**Inves Capital, the issuer of ZARP, is introducing ZARPY: a yield-bearing companion to the ZARP rand stablecoin. Approved partners can earn a share of top-tier money market fund returns for as long as their ZARP is staked. That can be a month, a day or an hour.**

## Short-term rand has always been left out

Money market funds are where South African institutions keep cash they want safe, liquid and earning. The best rates are in the institutional share classes, and those reward money that stays put. Subscriptions and redemptions run on business-day cut-offs and settlement cycles. Even newer digital alternatives usually count yield from a single daily snapshot. Rand that is needed again this afternoon, or that sits between trades overnight, usually earns little or nothing.

ZARPY changes that.

## How ZARPY works

- **Stake ZARP, receive ZARPY.** An approved partner stakes ZARP into the ZARPY smart contract on [Base](https://www.base.org/) and receives ZARPY one-for-one.
- **Yield accrues continuously.** From the moment ZARP is staked, ZARPY accrues yield in real time. The rate is benchmarked to the institutional class of a top-tier South African money market fund, less a service fee. Stake for an hour and you earn an hour's share of the day's yield.
- **Unstake whenever you like.** Return ZARPY at any time and get your ZARP back one-for-one, on-chain, immediately. There is no lock-up and no minimum term.
- **Claim on your schedule.** The yield you have earned is recorded for you automatically and is never forfeited. You claim it as additional ZARPY whenever it suits you.

## Why this is new

Rand-denominated yield is not new. Money market funds offer it, and rand stablecoins have started to as well. What ZARPY adds is time. Yield accrues second by second, not at an end-of-day snapshot or on a fund's settlement cycle, so capital that is idle for only a few hours still earns.

To our knowledge, ZARPY is the first product in South Africa to give rand that is staked for as little as an hour access to top-tier, institutional money market fund rates.

> Institutional treasuries have always had to choose between earning a proper money market rate and keeping their rand on hand. ZARPY removes that trade-off. Your rand earns for every hour it isn't working, and it's back in your wallet the moment you need it.
>
> — Simon Dingle, Managing Director, Inves Capital

## Built for institutions

ZARPY is a permissioned product for institutions, not a retail or open DeFi product. It is available only to ZARP partners, such as exchanges, treasury teams and funds, that Inves Capital has verified and separately approved for ZARPY. Approved partners may stake ZARP however they acquired it.

## Designed with safeguards

- **Fully backed.** Every ZARPY is backed one-for-one by ZARP. Inves Capital owns and manages the reserves behind ZARP and ZARPY under a conservative investment policy and bears any investment loss itself.
- **Yield tied to real returns.** The smart contract can create yield only up to the returns the reserves have actually earned, as confirmed by independent attestation.
- **Independently checked.** Reserves are [attested independently](/transparency), and the ZARPY smart contracts will have an independent security audit before general availability.

## What happens next

ZARPY is in a live pilot with approved partners on a Base test network. Launch on Base mainnet and general availability are targeted for later this quarter, before the end of 2026, once the security audit and legal review are complete. We will share full product terms at launch.

ZARP partners who want to take part should contact us at [hello@zarpstablecoin.com](mailto:hello@zarpstablecoin.com?subject=ZARPY). Not yet a partner? Start with a [partner application](/get-zarp#partner-application).
