## What Inves collects, and from whom

- **From the representative who applies:** the company's names, registration number, country, business activity and tax details; the representative's names, email address and phone number; and whether the representative holds a prominent public position, with its details.
- **During onboarding, from the company:** identification of its directors, shareholders, beneficial owners and representatives; sanctions, adverse-media and criminal-record screening of them; the company's source of funds; and a tax-residence self-certification for each controlling person.

**If you are a director, shareholder, beneficial owner or other controlling person, Inves collects your information from the company that applies, not from you.** The company should give you this notice.

## Why, and under which law

Inves is an accountable institution under the **Financial Intelligence Centre Act 38 of 2001** (FIC Act). The Act requires it to identify and verify its clients and the people who own or control them, to screen them, to keep records, and to report to the Financial Intelligence Centre (sections 21 to 21H, 22, 22A, 23, 28 and 29). Inves also collects tax-residence information to report to the South African Revenue Service under the Crypto-Asset Reporting Framework and the Common Reporting Standard. Inves uses the information to decide the application and, if it is approved, to perform its agreement with the company.

**Screening results are special personal information.** Inves uses them only to comply with the FIC Act and holds them itself.

## Required or voluntary

The fields marked as required are required by the FIC Act or needed for the agreement. If they are not given, Inves cannot consider the application. The other fields are voluntary.

## Outside South Africa

The platform keeps its data in South Africa. **Totemiq reaches it from the United Arab Emirates.** Inves relies on POPIA section 72(1)(a): Totemiq is bound by a written agreement to process it on principles substantially similar to POPIA's, to pass it on only with Inves's written consent to a recipient bound in the same way, and to give you your rights through Inves. The portal's access logs (your IP address, the page address and your browser details) are kept by Amazon Web Services in the United States for 30 days. If you complete the tax-residence self-certification form on zarpstablecoin.com, Formspree, in the United States, delivers it to Inves.

## How long

Inves keeps the records the FIC Act requires for at least five years from the end of the business relationship, and five years from each transaction, then de-identifies them. A declined application is kept for five years from the decision. Inves does not yet de-identify records automatically when their period ends; it is building the means to.

Inves answers every request subject to section 29 of the FIC Act, which forbids it to tell anyone that it has reported a matter to the Financial Intelligence Centre.
